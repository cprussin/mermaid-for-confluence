# Privacy policy: Offline Mermaid Diagrams for Confluence

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Offline Mermaid Diagrams for Confluence ("the app") is published by Connor Prussin, a sole proprietor based in California, USA ("we").

## What the app does with data

- **Diagram text:** saved by Confluence as the macro's configuration inside the page that contains it. It stays in your Atlassian site and is governed by your agreement with Atlassian.
- **Rendering:** happens in your browser with a Mermaid engine bundled in the app.
- **License status:** Atlassian tells the app at runtime whether your license is active. The app uses this only to decide whether to show the diagram and does not store it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data.
- It makes no network requests outside Atlassian.
- It uses no analytics, tracking, cookies, Forge storage or third-party services.
- It requests no Confluence API scopes.

## Sub-processors

None. The app runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform ("Runs on Atlassian").

## Changes

We will post any changes to this policy in this file. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
